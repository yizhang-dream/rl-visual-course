⑦ 合规提醒7 · Compliance note

⚠️
课程明确：禁止使用现成算法工具箱，算法必须从零自己写；「明显 LLM 生成的报告 / 代码最多扣到 0 分」。上方第 4 组社区仓库包含作业算法的完整实现（值迭代、策略迭代、MC、TD、DQN、REINFORCE、A2C…）——把它们当思路参照与调试对照，不要照抄结构或注释。 The course is explicit: ready-made algorithm toolboxes are forbidden and every algorithm must be written from scratch; "reports/code that are obviously LLM-generated can be graded down to 0". The community repos in group 4 contain complete implementations of the homework algorithms (value/policy iteration, MC, TD, DQN, REINFORCE, A2C…) — treat them as references for ideas and debugging, and do not copy their structure or comments.
